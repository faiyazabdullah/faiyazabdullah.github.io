I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Dhaka, Bangladesh. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (Major in Data Science) at United International University (UIU), graduating with a CGPA of 3.98/4.00. My undergraduate thesis, Translation Tangles: Performance Benchmarking and Bias Detection in LLM-Based Translation Across Language Families and Domains, was defended in July 2025 and later extended into a paper accepted at the Findings of ACL 2026.

My research sits at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Multimodal Machine Learning, and Computer Vision. I am particularly interested in how large language and vision–language models behave beyond English — their reasoning, cultural grounding, hallucination behaviour, and the performance gaps they carry across language families, dialects, and low-resource settings. Alongside this, I build multimodal datasets and benchmarks for real-world problems such as public-health surveillance, accessibility, and online harm detection.

My work has appeared at ACL, EMNLP, EACL, ICLR (Tiny Papers, Notable — Top 5%), *SEM, CVPR and NeurIPS workshops, as well as in journals including IEEE Access, Data in Brief (Elsevier) and Applied Sciences (MDPI). I also serve the community as a reviewer for ACL Rolling Review, *SEM 2026, and several Springer Nature journals. Previously I was a Research Assistant at the MIRA Wing, CCD, Independent University Bangladesh, an AI Intern at Ontik Technology, and a Teaching Assistant and Grader at UIU. A full record is available in my CV and on Google Scholar.

I am actively looking for PhD opportunities in NLP and multimodal machine learning. If our interests overlap, I would be glad to hear from you — feel free to reach out.

Away from papers and datasets, you will most likely find me watching football. A lifelong Real Madrid supporter — Hala Madrid, siempre!

    Vision–language models, cultural and dialectal grounding, and multimodal datasets that pair perception with textual reasoning.

    Detection and segmentation for real-world problems in public health, accessibility, and resource-constrained deployment.
