Hello, I am Md. Faiyaz Abdullah Sayeedi. I completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (Major in Data Science) at United International University (UIU), Bangladesh, graduating with a GPA of 3.98/4.0. During my undergraduate years I served as an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant at UIU, supporting students and faculty in Artificial Intelligence, Data Structures and Algorithms, and Introduction to Computer Systems.

I am currently a Research Assistant at BRAC University, where I work on AI-Powered Contradiction and Conflict Detection in Evolving Documents, developing Natural Language Processing pipelines that identify logical inconsistencies and temporal conflicts across document versions. Previously, I was a Research Assistant at the Medical Imaging Research & Analysis (MIRA) Wing, Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS), Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB), and an AI Intern at Ontik Technology. My research interests include, but are not limited to, Natural Language Processing, Multilingualism, Responsible AI, and Multimodal Learning.

I have published several peer-reviewed papers in reputable international workshops, conferences, and journals such as ACL, EMNLP, EACL, ICLR (Tiny Papers Track), *SEM, CVPR Workshops, NeurIPS Workshop (MusIML), IEEE Access, and Data in Brief (Elsevier). My undergraduate thesis, Translation Tangles: Performance Benchmarking and Bias Detection in LLM-Based Translation Across Language Families and Domains, was supervised by Swakkhar Shatabda, Md. Mofijul Islam, and Tasnim Mohiuddin. I also serve as a reviewer for ACL Rolling Review (ARR), *SEM 2026, and several Springer Nature journals. Check out my Google Scholar profile for more details.

When I am not immersed in code, datasets, or late-night paper submissions, I am likely cheering for my favorite football club. As a die-hard fan of Real Madrid, I follow every match with the same passion I bring to my research— Hala Madrid, siempre!
